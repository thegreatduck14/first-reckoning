How to Play

You and the AI each start with one city, a Harvester, a Scout, and a Settler on a fogged, procedurally generated island map — every new game reshuffles the terrain, so scout before you commit. Harvesters parked on a goods tile add production; that shared production pool (plus a bonus for every extra city you found) funds your build queue, while research slowly unlocks stronger units and buildings. Move units around the grid and click an enemy unit or base within reach to see a Combat Analysis of damage dealt and taken before you confirm the attack — infantry counters vehicles, vehicles hit hard in the open, artillery is built to crack bases, and flyers ignore terrain entirely. Settlers found new cities on open, non-mountain ground away from existing ones; Frigates, Carriers, and Battleships (built from a coastal Shipyard) ferry land units across water to other islands. Scattered native camps are neutral until a Scout stands next to one — ask for peace to make that camp permanently safe, or strike a war plan (handing over a spare infantry unit as tribute) to turn its garrison into an ally that fights the AI for you. Whoever loses every city loses the game; end your turn with the End Turn button once you've moved, built, and attacked for the round.
